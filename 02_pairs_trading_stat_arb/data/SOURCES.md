# Data sources

## fx_daily_fred.csv
- Origin: US Federal Reserve H.10 daily exchange rates (as published on FRED),
  packaged by the datahub.io "exchange-rates" dataset:
  https://github.com/datasets/exchange-rates, `data/daily.csv`,
  commit 0947c2304370a5a4dd8db74cdacb8486e7d85cbe (2026-09-29).
- That file quotes every currency as units per US dollar. Converted here to
  market convention: EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD = 1 / (units per USD);
  USDCAD unchanged. Rows from 2000-01-03; US holidays are blank.
- Spot checks: EURUSD 1.592 and GBPUSD 2.004 on 2008-07-15 (near their 2008 highs).
- SHA-256 of the converted file is recorded in `CHECKSUMS.txt`.
